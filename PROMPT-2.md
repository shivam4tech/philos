## 3. NIETZSCHE × DELEUZE — AGAIN / DIFFERENCE CONTAMINATION

Standard recurrence repeats the recorded life sequence.

Contaminated recurrence repeats it while tiny differences emerge from repetition.

## 4. HEIDEGGER × MARX — BROKEN TOOL / COMMODITY CONTAMINATION

The equipment first appears through use.

When it breaks, it becomes conspicuous.

Then expose another dimension:

Who made it? Under what relation? Who owns it? What was abstracted when it appeared simply as a commodity/tool?

Make the transition mechanical, not textual.

---

## 5. WITTGENSTEIN × DERRIDA — PRIVATE ROOM / TRACE CONTAMINATION

The private symbol gradually receives rules through use.

But each stabilizing rule depends on further contextual distinctions.

Create tension between: public criteria and indefinite differential articulation.

Do not pretend this represents an easy reconciliation of the thinkers.

Explicitly say in the post-machine apparatus that the hybrid is an experimental confrontation, not a claim that the positions are equivalent.

---

## 6. LEIBNIZ × FOUCAULT — NO WINDOWS / OBSERVATION CONTAMINATION

Nobody can access another monad's inner state.

Yet the institution predicts every monad perfectly from observable correspondence.

Ask:

IS PERFECT PREDICTABILITY EQUIVALENT TO ACCESS?

Turn this into an eerie simulated analytics experiment.

# PART VII — THE ARCHIVE SHOULD BECOME A REAL PHILOSOPHICAL OBJECT

Improve every textual apparatus.

Use a consistent structure:

1. PRIMARY SOURCE
2. CONCEPTUAL PROBLEM
3. WHAT THE MACHINE DID
4. WHAT THE MACHINE DISTORTED
5. WHY THAT DISTORTION MATTERS
6. COMPETING READING
7. QUESTIONS TO TAKE BACK TO THE TEXT
8. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

This section should explicitly acknowledge that a software mechanic is an interpretation. That is philosophically important.

Example:

"This apparatus treats eternal recurrence primarily as a practical test of affirmation. That is not the only interpretation of §341."

Good.

Bad: "Nietzsche believed we should live without regrets."
